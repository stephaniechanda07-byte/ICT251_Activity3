## About the website

This is my personal student portfolio. It introduces me, my hobbies, my weekly web development learning plan, my projects, my photos and my media (a short video and an audio recording). It is built with plain HTML5, CSS and JavaScript and is published on Render through GitHub.

## Sections

About Me, My Hobbies, My Learning Plan, My Projects, My Photos, My Media and Contact.

## JavaScript features and how to test them

1. **Contact form validation and preview**
   - Leave the fields empty, enter only spaces, or enter an email like `abc@` and click Validate. Errors appear beside the fields.
   - Enter a valid name, email and message. A summary appears saying the data was validated. No message is sent (browser demonstration only).

2. **Gallery viewer**
   - In My Photos, click Next and Previous to change the photo and its caption.
   - Previous is disabled on the first photo and Next is disabled on the last photo.

3. **Theme switch**
   - Click the theme button at the top of the page to switch between light and dark mode.
   - The choice is remembered when the page is refreshed.

4. **Study hours calculator**
   - In My Learning Plan, enter hours per day and days per week (for example 2 and 5) to see 10 hours per week.
   - Blank, non-numeric or negative hours, and days outside 1 to 7, show an error message.
